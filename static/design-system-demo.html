Usage Guidelines

✅ DO

  • Use Savanna Gold for primary CTAs
  • Use grays for 90% of the UI
  • Use green sparingly for badges and icons
  • Keep backgrounds light and clean
  • Use generous white space

❌ DON'T

  • Don't use green for large areas
  • Don't use multiple bright colors together
  • Don't fill every space with color
  • Don't make everything "pop"
