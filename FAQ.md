# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This document addresses common inquiries regarding the SafariSmart Kenya platform, its functionality, and technical details.

---

## General Information

### Is the platform free to use?
Yes, account creation and itinerary generation are currently free of charge. The platform is designed as a public utility to promote tourism.

### Do I need an account to view destinations?
No. The destination gallery is publicly accessible. However, an account is required to generate, save, and manage personalized itineraries.

---

## Itinerary Generation

### How are the budget estimates calculated?
The provided budget estimates are approximations based on average market rates for accommodation, park entry fees, and standard transport costs. These figures are subject to change based on seasonality, availability, and specific service provider rates. Users are advised to verify actual costs before booking.

### Can I book directly through the platform?
Currently, SafariSmart Kenya operates solely as a planning and recommendation engine. Direct booking functionality is not yet implemented. Users should contact the recommended lodges or tour operators directly.

### Why does the AI suggest unselected destinations?
The AI algorithm prioritizes a logical and enjoyable travel flow. If a selected destination is geographically isolated or incompatible with the user's budget and time constraints, the AI may suggest alternative or additional locations to optimize the itinerary.

---

## Account and Security

### How do I reset my password?
Navigate to the login page and select the "Forgot Password" link. Follow the instructions sent to your registered email address to securely reset your credentials.

### How is my data protected?
User data is protected using industry-standard security measures. Passwords are hashed using Argon2, and all database connections are secured. We strictly adhere to data privacy principles and do not share personal information with unauthorized third parties.

## Technical Support

### The application is not loading correctly on my mobile device.
The platform is optimized for responsive display. If issues persist, please ensure your mobile browser is updated to the latest version and try clearing your browser cache.
